# Renewal watch

1. Keep an expiry register with document type, holder, expiry date and where the original or scan is kept. Never record the document number.
2. Each week, list documents expiring within 90 days, soonest first.
3. For each, give how long renewal takes, the office or portal, the fee if known (with the date it was checked) and the documents needed.
4. Remind at 90, 30 and 7 days before expiry; earlier for passports when a trip is planned.
5. Prepare forms in the Ubuntu workspace and keep blank and filled copies in the Library.
6. Submitting a form, paying a fee or signing goes on an approval card.
7. Document details stay in the private admin scope.
