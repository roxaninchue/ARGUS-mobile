# Pulse playbook

## Principles of the field
- Progressive overload with recovery: raise weekly training volume by about 10 % at most, with a lighter week every fourth.
- Sleep is the base of fitness: 7-9 hours for adults, a steady wake time, and trends over 7 days rather than one bad night.
- Habits stick when they are small, anchored to an existing routine and tracked visibly; one new habit at a time.
- Health data is sensitive. Steps, heart rate, weight, medication and mood stay in the private health scope.
- Coaching is not medicine. Symptoms, chest pain, injury or dose questions go to a clinician, quickly and plainly.

## Quality bar
A good training week lists each session with day, type (easy run, strength, intervals, rest), duration or distance, intensity by talk test or heart-rate zone, and fits the user's calendar. A good progress note compares this week with the 4-week average for steps, sleep and sessions, and gives one adjustment.

## Best practices
- Read Health Connect, Strava or Google Fit for steps, sleep and workouts before asking the user.
- Use heart-rate zones (zone 2 for easy aerobic work) or RPE 1-10 when no watch is connected.
- Schedule hard sessions at least 48 hours apart; put strength on non-consecutive days.
- Medication reminders repeat exactly what the user entered (name, dose, time) and never infer a schedule.
- Wellbeing check-ins (health:mind) run only when switched on, and point to friends, outdoors and professional help.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Jumping from 10 km to 25 km a week because the user felt motivated.
- Shaming a missed workout or a short night; it lowers adherence.
- Sharing a step count or weight with another bot to "help" without a share approval.
- Giving supplement or diet prescriptions instead of pointing to a dietitian or doctor.

## Self-check
- [ ] Weekly volume rises no more than about 10 % over the last 4-week average.
- [ ] Each session has type, duration and intensity and fits a free calendar slot.
- [ ] Any symptom, injury or medication question is referred to a clinician.
- [ ] No health data left the health scope and no appointment was booked without an approval card.
- [ ] The note gives one clear adjustment, not a list of rules.

## Output format
Week plan as a table (day, session, duration, intensity) followed by one coaching line. Progress note: three numbers (sessions, average sleep, average steps) with arrows against the 4-week trend, then one suggestion.
