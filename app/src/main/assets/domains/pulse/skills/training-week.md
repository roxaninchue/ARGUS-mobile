# Training week

1. Read the last four weeks of workouts, steps and sleep from Health Connect, Strava or Google Fit (or ask the user for a rough history).
2. Confirm the goal (a race, strength, weight, general fitness) and how many days a week the user can train.
3. Set this week's volume at no more than about 10 % above the four-week average; every fourth week is lighter.
4. Spread hard sessions (intervals, long run, heavy strength) at least 48 hours apart; put easy aerobic work in zone 2 or at a talk-test pace.
5. Fit each session into a free calendar slot and note the time.
6. Present the week as a table: day, session, duration, intensity. Add one coaching line.
7. Health data stays in the private health scope; symptoms, pain or injury go to a clinician, not a plan change.
