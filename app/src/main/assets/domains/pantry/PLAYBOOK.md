# Pantry playbook

## Principles of the field
- Allergies and dietary rules are hard constraints: they are checked against every ingredient, sauce and garnish, every time.
- Meal planning saves money when it reuses ingredients across days and plans for leftovers.
- Balanced plates: a protein, a vegetable or two, a starch such as nshima, rice or potatoes, and fruit across the day.
- Shopping lists follow the store's layout so one trip covers the week.
- Usual orders are preferences with context: the office lunch order is not the Friday-night family order.

## Quality bar
A good meal plan lists each dinner with dish name, cook time, servings and the shared ingredients, and it respects every dietary rule. A good grocery list gives quantities in units the store sells, grouped by aisle, with items already at home removed. A delivery order names the restaurant, items, total and expected arrival.

## Best practices
- Check user:core allergies and dietary lines before suggesting any dish or restaurant.
- Plan 5 dinners for a 7-day week; leave 2 for leftovers or eating out.
- Batch-cook bases (beans, relish, stews) on the weekend to cut weekday cook time under 30 minutes.
- Use seasonal local produce (rape, tomatoes, okra, mangoes in season) for price and freshness.
- Save each confirmed delivery order with its platform, restaurant and occasion to offer again later.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Suggesting a peanut sauce to someone with a nut allergy because the recipe title hid it.
- Grocery lists in grams for items sold by the bunch or the tray.
- Placing an order or checking out without an approval card, or reusing an old address silently.
- Repeating the same three dinners every week.

## Self-check
- [ ] Every dish was checked against the user's allergies and dietary rules.
- [ ] Ingredients are shared across at least two meals where possible.
- [ ] The grocery list is grouped by aisle with sellable quantities.
- [ ] No order or checkout happened without an approval card.
- [ ] Cook times are stated and weekday dinners stay realistic.

## Output format
Meal plan table (day, dinner, cook time, servings). Then the grocery list grouped by aisle with quantities. For orders: restaurant, items, total, arrival estimate, then the approval card.
