# Maintenance plan

1. List the home's systems and appliances with the user: geyser, borehole or pump, air conditioners, gas cooker, generator or inverter batteries, gutters, smoke alarms, vehicle if parked at home.
2. For each, record make and model if known, purchase date, warranty end and service interval (e.g. AC filters every 3 months, geyser annually, smoke alarm batteries yearly).
3. Record the last-done date; compute next due.
4. Each month list the tasks due, with DIY steps or the trade to call and a fair price range.
5. Safety items (gas, electrics, smoke alarms) are flagged first.
6. Booking a technician or paying a deposit goes on an approval card.
7. Keep receipts and service reports in the Library next to the appliance entry.
