# Exam countdown

1. Get each exam's date, subject, syllabus or topic list, and the user's confidence per topic (1-5).
2. Weight topics by syllabus share and low confidence; weak, heavy topics get the most sessions.
3. Schedule 25-45 minute sessions per day until the exam, interleaving topics and leaving one review day a week.
4. Turn each studied topic into flashcards in the Learn view with spaced intervals (1, 3, 7, 14 days).
5. Put timed past papers in the final two weeks; mark against the memo and move weak questions into cards.
6. Each morning, tell the user days left and today's topic; stay silent on days with nothing planned.
7. Help explain and practise; never write graded work for submission.
