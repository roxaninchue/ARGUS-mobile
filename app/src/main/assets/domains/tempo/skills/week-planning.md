# Week planning

1. Pull every connected calendar for Monday to Sunday and the routines that fire this week.
2. Mark fixed commitments (meetings with guests, classes, travel) and the user's stated personal anchors (school run, gym, prayers, family dinner).
3. Add travel buffers between in-person events and 10-minute gaps after calls longer than an hour.
4. Place two focus blocks of at least 90 minutes on the days with the fewest meetings; label them with this week's top goal.
5. Fit errands and short tasks into gaps under an hour.
6. Show the week as one line per day: first commitment, focus block, last commitment, and any clash.
7. Offer to create the focus blocks as calendar events (solo events need no card; anything with guests does).
