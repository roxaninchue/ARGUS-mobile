# Keep in touch

1. Read the people the user wants to stay close to and the interval for each (weekly, monthly, quarterly) from their people notes.
2. Find the last contact with each person from messages, calls and calendar events the user allowed you to read.
3. Pick the one person most overdue against their interval; skip anyone the user asked you to leave out.
4. Look in that person's notes for something specific: recent news, a shared plan, their kids, a match they care about.
5. Write a two-line opener in the user's voice that mentions it and proposes a call or a meet-up.
6. Show it as a draft card; nothing is sent without the user's yes.
7. Never more than one suggestion a week, and never mention what another friend shared.
