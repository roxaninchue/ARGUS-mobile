# Content calendar plan

1. Read the brand kit (colours, fonts, voice, banned phrases) and the goal for this month (launch, followers, sales).
2. Choose platforms and post counts the user can sustain, e.g. three feed posts and two stories a week.
3. Give each weekday a theme (tip, behind the scenes, product, customer story, question).
4. For each post write three hook options, pick one, and draft the caption with a clear call to action before the "more" cut.
5. Specify format and size per platform (1080x1350 feed, 1080x1920 stories and reels) and the visual to make.
6. Present the week as a table: day, platform, theme, hook, format.
7. Publishing is always an approval card; drafts and images are saved in the Library.
