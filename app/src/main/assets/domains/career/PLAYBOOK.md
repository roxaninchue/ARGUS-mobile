# Career playbook

## Principles of the field
- Hiring managers scan a CV in under a minute: impact first, quantified, in reverse-chronological order, on one or two pages.
- Every application is tailored. Mirror the job description's must-have skills with true evidence from the user's history.
- Networking is giving before asking: a specific, short message referencing shared context outperforms a generic connection request.
- Career growth is planned in quarters: a target role, the skill gaps to close and a visible piece of work per gap.
- Meeting preparation decides the meeting: goal, attendees' interests and the decision needed.

## Quality bar
A good CV bullet starts with a strong verb, states the result with a number and names the tool or method ("Cut CI time 40 % by caching Gradle builds"). A good application packet has a tailored CV, a cover letter under 250 words and the deadline tracked. Good meeting prep fits on one screen.

## Best practices
- Keep a master CV with every achievement; derive role-specific versions from it.
- Use the STAR shape (situation, task, action, result) for interview stories; prepare five that cover leadership, conflict, failure, impact and learning.
- Track applications with company, role, date sent, contact, stage and next follow-up date.
- Research the company's recent news, product and interviewer's public profile before an interview.
- Follow up one week after an application or interview with a two-line note.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Listing duties instead of results.
- Inventing or inflating titles, dates, degrees or numbers: a CV must be true.
- Sending outreach or submitting an application without the user's approval card.
- One CV for every role, with keywords the job never asked for.

## Self-check
- [ ] Each bullet has a verb, a measurable result and a method.
- [ ] Every claim matches something the user actually told the team.
- [ ] The application lists its deadline and next follow-up date.
- [ ] No application, outreach or profile post went out without an approval card.
- [ ] Interview prep includes at least three STAR stories and two questions to ask.

## Output format
For CVs: the rewritten section in markdown, then a short change list. For applications: a table (company, role, stage, deadline, next step). For meeting prep: goal, people, last contact, two questions.
