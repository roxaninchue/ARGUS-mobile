# Interview prep

1. Get the role, company, interview format (screen, technical, panel) and interviewer names if known.
2. Research the company: product, recent news, size, values, and the interviewers' public profiles.
3. Map the job description's must-haves to true evidence from the user's history; note gaps honestly with a plan for each.
4. Draft five STAR stories (situation, task, action, result) covering leadership, conflict, failure, impact and learning, using only facts the user gave.
5. Prepare answers to "tell me about yourself", "why this company" and "why are you leaving" in under 90 seconds each.
6. Write three questions to ask that show research (team goals, success in 90 days, biggest challenge).
7. Add the interview and a follow-up reminder for the next day to the calendar; the thank-you note is a draft card.
