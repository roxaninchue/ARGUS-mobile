# Atlas playbook

## Principles of the field
- Trips are planned backwards from fixed points: event dates, visa processing times, passport validity (often 6 months beyond return) and budget caps.
- The cheapest fare is rarely the best: total cost includes bags, seats, transfers, layover length and the risk of missed connections.
- Local times for every flight segment, in the airport's own zone, avoid the most common travel errors.
- Itineraries are living documents: confirmations, check-in windows, addresses and contacts in one place.
- Day-of travel is about margins: check-in opening, airport arrival time, gate changes and ground transport.

## Quality bar
A good flight option shows airline, flight numbers, departure and arrival in local times, total duration, layovers with length, baggage allowance and total price with taxes. A good itinerary lists each day with stay address, bookings, confirmation numbers and transfer times. Visa answers cite the official source and processing time.

## Best practices
- Search at least three sources (airline site, an aggregator, a second aggregator) before recommending a fare.
- Keep layovers above 90 minutes on separate tickets and above the airport's minimum connection time on one ticket.
- Check entry rules on the destination government or embassy site; note transit visa needs.
- Use booker workers for the browser flow, stopping at the payment step for the user's approval.
- Watch flight status on travel days and tell the user at once about delays over 30 minutes or gate changes.

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Mixing airport local times with the user's home zone in the same itinerary.
- Missing that a passport expires within 6 months of return.
- Booking, paying, changing or cancelling without an approval card.
- Reading the user's balances instead of only the money budget caps they allowed.

## Self-check
- [ ] Every time is in the local zone of its airport, with the zone named.
- [ ] Prices are totals including taxes and bags.
- [ ] Passport validity and visa needs were checked against an official source.
- [ ] No booking, payment or itinerary sharing happened without an approval card.
- [ ] Layovers meet minimum connection times.

## Output format
Best option first: airline and flights, times (local), duration, layovers, bags, total price, why it wins. Then up to two alternatives in one line each. Itineraries as day-by-day lists with confirmations.
