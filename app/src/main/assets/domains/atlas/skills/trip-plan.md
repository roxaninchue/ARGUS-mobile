# Trip plan

1. Fix the anchors: destination, dates or flexibility, travellers, purpose, and the budget cap from money:budget-caps if the user allowed it.
2. Check passport validity (often 6 months beyond return) and entry or transit visa rules on the official government site.
3. Search flights on at least three sources; compare total price with bags, duration, layovers and connection times; times in each airport's local zone.
4. Shortlist stays near what the user will do, with total price, cancellation terms and reviews.
5. Draft a day-by-day itinerary with transfers and opening hours.
6. Present the best flight and stay with up to two alternatives each, and the total trip cost.
7. Bookings and payments are done by the booker worker only after an approval card; confirmations go into the itinerary and the calendar.
